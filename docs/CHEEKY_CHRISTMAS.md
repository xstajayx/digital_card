## Artwork and provenance

The six covers were generated specifically for this collection using text-only prompts, reviewed as individual scenes, cropped and compressed to local 504 × 504 WebP files. The cover lettering remains editable text in the card renderer. The interior line art and new clock and wave stamps are original SVG drawings made for this change. No third-party photography, illustration, logos, source images or stock licences were used for the new artwork. The site uses its existing bundled fonts and does not need an image service to display or share these cards.

The visual review checked vehicle silhouettes, prop details, character shapes, costumes and scene composition for obvious conflicts. The clock-town courier uses an open cloth canopy and brass ski runners. The space courier uses an open copper crescent with parcels strapped inside. The workshop uses a kettle-shaped Santa with a bead-light beard and round helpers. The harbour creature is a friendly whiskered animal with a curled fin. The six titles and greetings are the supplied original festive wordplay. This visual review is limited to obvious similarity and does not establish legal clearance.

The design records use the existing v5 link format. New IDs add six records to the 121-design baseline. The existing inside-decoration setting, paper and font choices, and folded viewer apply to each one.

The six Christmas designs and the six earlier Cheeky & Clever designs passed the Chromium catalogue, editor and shared recipient journeys at 320, 375, 390 and 1440 px. The new cards passed heading-fit, artwork, keyboard toggle, wax seal, interior and maker’s mark checks. All 74 Node tests passed.
