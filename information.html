Silent Hello

A few important details.

Owner review required before launch. These notices describe this static release; they are not a completed legal policy for a trading business.

Privacy

We do not operate a card database, analytics or AI service in this release. A share link contains its message, and anyone with the link can read it. Older query links may appear in hosting access logs; newer fragment links keep card content out of HTTP requests. GitHub Pages processes ordinary hosting requests under its own policies.

Drafts are saved only when you press Save draft, in this browser’s local storage. Delete draft removes the saved copy; closing a tab does not delete it. Avoid sensitive information on shared devices.

Cookies and local storage

No analytics, advertising or non-essential cookies are added by this application. Optional draft storage is user initiated. Hosting providers may have their own practices.

Terms

The current editor is free and has no checkout. Only share content you have permission to use. Links are transferable and are not private access controls. Store-backed expiry, revocation and refunds are not available.

Contact and reporting

The owner must supply a verified support address, business identity and abuse-reporting route before public commercial launch. No address or registration number has been invented. Without storage-backed links, we cannot revoke an individual card URL; ask its sender to stop distributing it. Hosting abuse can be reported to the hosting provider.

AI and refunds

Photos can be processed locally in your browser and explicitly saved in IndexedDB on this device. They are not uploaded or shared. Clearing browser data may remove photo drafts. No AI prompts or payments are collected; persistent media sharing needs a separately reviewed service. Refund terms must be reviewed before any sale is enabled.
