About Silent Hello

How it works

Choose an illustrated card, a single flower or a bouquet.

Make it personal

Add your own message and preview the gift before sending.

Send a little smile

Copy the complete link, use native share or send through WhatsApp.

A few little questions.

Do I need an account or payment?

No. The current collection and editor are free to use without registering.

Where is my message stored?

The shared link contains the gift and its message. Anyone with the link can read it. Optional local drafts stay in this browser until you delete them.

Will my old card link work?

Yes. Previously supported v1–v5 card links remain supported.

Can I add a photo to a card?

Photos can be edited and saved as local previews in the card creator. Photo sharing uses the separate hosted service when configured. Illustrated cards and digital flowers share by link alone.
